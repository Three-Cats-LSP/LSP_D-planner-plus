**Post**

Hi everyone,

I released **LSP D-Planner+ v3.0.1**, a free open-source decompression planning app for recreational and technical divers.

Live app:
https://threecats-lsp.com/d-planner-plus/

GitHub:
https://github.com/Three-Cats-LSP/LSP_D-planner-plus

Android APK:
https://threecats-lsp.com/d-planner-plus/download.html

It runs fully in the browser, works offline after first load, installs as a PWA, and is also available as a direct Android APK. No account, no ads, no Play Store.

Main features:

- Buhlmann ZHL-16C + Gradient Factors
- VPM-B and VPM-B/GFS
- PADI RDP recreational table mode
- OC, CCR, and pSCR planning
- Trimix bottom, travel, and deco gases
- Travel gas MinOD/MOD and switch-depth handling
- MOD blocking and gas-safety warnings
- Contingency planning for gas loss, extra bottom time, and deeper-than-planned scenarios
- Per-cylinder gas consumption cards with remaining gas bars
- Canonical gas labels: Air, 100%, 50/00, 18/45, etc.
- Dive graph, schedule table, tissue view, gas tools, MOD/END/EAD/best-mix calculators
- TXT, copy, slate, and PDF export

This 3.0.1 release is a major cleanup after the 2.5 line. A lot changed under the hood:

- extracted runtime modules out of the old large HTML file
- cleaner ZHL and VPM engine boundaries
- new risk-first audit pass focused on engines and safety paths first
- redesigned schedule table and gas-consumption UI
- stronger browser, engine, export, and Android release gates

Safety note: this is a planning aid, not a dive computer and not a replacement for training, tables, team procedures, or independent verification. Please verify plans with your own tools and training before using anything in the water.

Feedback is welcome, especially from divers who use CCR, pSCR, trimix, VPM, or contingency planning workflows. Bug reports and suggestions are very helpful.
